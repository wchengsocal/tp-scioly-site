# PRODUCT.md — Torrey Pines Science Olympiad

## What this is
A public website for Torrey Pines High School's Science Olympiad team (Division C), a
student-run competitive science team in San Diego, California.

## Mechanism
Students test into events, specialize, and compete a season-long circuit of invitational
tournaments leading to a regional and then the California state tournament. The team runs
itself: students build, code, study, and lab; coaches advise.

## Audience & mode
Primary surface mode: **Persuade**.

1. **Prospective members (leads the page).** TPHS students deciding whether to try out.
   Primary action: sign up for tryouts.
2. **Parents & sponsors (real second track, lower).** Adults deciding whether to donate,
   sponsor, or buy merch. Secondary action: support the team.

## Product truth (verified from tpscienceolympiad.wixsite.com/scioly, Aug 2026)
- Torrey Pines High School, San Diego, CA. Division C.
- **A student-AND-PARENT run club** — not purely student-run. Welcomes students
  interested in STEM careers.
- 22 events this season, in three kinds: study (9), lab (7), build (6).
- **Entry is an application, not a tryout test.** There is an information meeting,
  then an application deadline, then teams are released.
- **Multiple teams of 15 students each.** Your team is you, your partners, and
  their partners.
- Each student competes in **3–4 events**, with **1–2 partners per event**.
- Events and partners are assigned from: application preferences, schedule
  compatibility, competition-day timing, past expertise, and partner dynamics.
- Weekly meetings per event, with **attendance tracked**. Students also study
  independently and with partners; build events need extra construction/testing time.
- Competition is a **full day**. **Medals for top-20 placements**; team trophies
  awarded on overall performance.
- **Materials are fully reimbursed** for relevant, reasonable purchases with
  receipts turned in to the coordinators.
- All invitationals on the calendar are **MiniSO** competitions.
